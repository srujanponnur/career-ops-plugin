# Employer vetting method

## Layoffs (2022 → today)
Sources: layoffs.fyi, TechCrunch/CNBC/Reuters/Bloomberg/GeekWire trackers, WARN-notice databases (warntracker.com), company 8-Ks and 10-Ks.
For each employer, record:
- Every round: date, headcount, % of workforce, functions hit. **Was engineering cut?**
- Headcount trend (stockanalysis.com and 10-Ks for public companies).
- Pattern: one-time correction vs recurring (e.g. "cuts every January") — the distinction is the whole point.
- Acquisitions followed by cuts to the acquired staff (Broadcom/VMware, Oracle/Cerner pattern). Never hold a pending green card in an acquired unit.
- Current signal: guidance cuts, activist pressure, CEO change, stated flat-headcount policy, hiring freeze.
- Note that revenue growth is no longer protective — companies cut while growing.

## Green card (only if the candidate needs sponsorship)
- **PERM (ETA-9089) volume** per fiscal year from DOL disclosure data via Ellis (ellis.com/visa-sponsors/<entity>/perm), MyVisaJobs, h1bdata.info. PERM, not H-1B LCA count, predicts an I-140. Compute the PERM:LCA ratio — a large gap is the warning.
- **DOL lag:** cases are indexed by decision date and analyst review runs 12–15+ months, so the most recent 1–2 fiscal years are structurally incomplete. Compare the last complete years. Only treat a low recent number as a slowdown if earlier years corroborate it.
- **Filing entity matters:** big employers file under several legal entities (e.g. "Atlassian US, Inc." vs "Atlassian, Inc."). A near-zero entity page can be the wrong entity.
- **Wait before PERM starts** — the single most decision-relevant fact, usually only in employee reports (Blind, Reddit r/h1b and r/USCIS, Glassdoor, attorney blogs). Record day-1 / 6 / 12 / 24 months / case-by-case and the source quality.
- Certification, denial and withdrawal rates. A spike in denials (one year with dozens) or mass withdrawals is a red flag.
- Budget reality: roughly 18–24 months from PERM kickoff to I-140 filing at current DOL speeds. Compare with the candidate's remaining visa years — a long tenure gate can consume most of it.
- Watch for non-competes and single-site in-office rules that would trap the candidate if the process stalls.
- Not publicly findable: EB-2 vs EB-3 practice and premium-processing policy. Say so; don't guess. AC21 portability is statutory — the employer controls only early I-140 withdrawal.

## Also check
- Any explicit "we do not sponsor" / "without the need for current or future sponsorship" language in the employer's postings.
- Citizenship or clearance requirements (defense-adjacent roles) — hard exclusion for non-citizens.

## Rating guide
- **Very stable:** no layoffs in the window, headcount growing.
- **Stable:** one small or unit-level round, growing or flat.
- **Mixed:** one meaningful round or rolling small cuts, flat headcount.
- **Risky:** recurring rounds or engineering cuts in the last 12 months.
- **Avoid:** structural decline, repeated large rounds, or acquired-unit gutting.
