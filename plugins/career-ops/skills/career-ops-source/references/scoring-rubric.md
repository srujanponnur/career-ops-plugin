# Scoring rubric (default, out of 100)

| Factor | Points | How to judge |
|---|---|---|
| Skills overlap with the real record | 40 | Named musts in the JD vs bullets in cv.md. Primary-language match matters most. |
| Level and years fit | 15 | Inside floor/ceiling; years asked vs years had. |
| Location | 15 | Local hybrid > remote > relocation. |
| Sponsorship posture | 10 | Silent/positive > "eligible to work" (ask recruiter) > explicit refusal (drop). |
| Comp vs floor | 10 | Band midpoint vs base floor. |
| Stage / pace / brand fit | 10 | Per the user's stated preference this time. |

## Variant for "well-known, high-paying, stable, green-card track record"
Layoff resilience 35 · green-card track record 35 · comp and brand 20 · fit and location 10. Requires career-ops-vet-employer data.

## Hard filters (drop, don't score)
Above level ceiling · more than max_years_required · explicit no-sponsorship (if needed) · citizenship/clearance required · already applied / in re-apply window.

## Honesty rules
- Every entry names one specific overlap using the candidate's real numbers and one real gap.
- "Stretch" is fine to recommend if the substance match is unusually specific — say so.
